PRIVACY POLICY — NexaBot Agency

Effective: October 1, 2026

1. Information we collect

Contact details, business information, payment references, support messages and knowledge-base content you submit through the portal.

2. Knowledge base & AI confidentiality

Your content is stored in isolated stores and is never used to train shared models, except where you choose to connect a third-party AI provider (e.g. Google Gemini, OpenAI), under their terms.

3. Public order notifications

When you place an order, a notification showing your country and — only if you opt in — your first name may appear on our website. You can change this any time in Profile.

4. Payments

Payments run through PayPal and bank transfer. We do not store card numbers.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Accounts use Firebase Authentication with email verification.

1. Scope & SLA

We build, train and deploy your chatbot within 48 business hours after payment confirmation and knowledge-base submission.

Standard setup is $1,099; the promo rate of $999 applies to orders placed during the promotion period. Maintenance is $149/month, billed from month 2. Included: WhatsApp Business API, Facebook Messenger, Facebook Catalog and Google Gemini. Each additional integration is +$49. Instagram is not supported.

3. Third-party fees

Meta WhatsApp conversation charges and third-party usage fees (e.g. OpenAI, Twilio) are paid by the client directly to those providers.
